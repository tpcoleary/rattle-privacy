Privacy Policy — Rattle

Effective date: 7 November 2026
App: Rattle (com.rattle.app)
Developer: Timothy O'Leary
Contact: timothypcoleary@gmail.com

Rattle is a personal reminder and logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Summary

  • Rattle is designed to keep your data on your device.
  • The developer does not operate a Rattle cloud account, analytics backend, advertising network, or data-sale pipeline for this app.
  • The phone app does not request the Android INTERNET permission.
  • Medication names, care-person names, alarm labels, and similar sensitive text you enter are stored in an on-device database and encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Optional features (notifications, exact alarms, biometric unlock, custom alarm sounds, Wear OS pairing) use Android / Google Play services APIs on your devices only.

2. Information you provide and store on the device

Depending on how you use Rattle, the app may store on your device:

CategoryExamples
Health / medication information you enter Medication names, dose schedules, taken / skipped / delayed history, medication notes
Care / caregiving information you enter Care-person names and roles, care task schedules and completion history
Personal organization data To-do titles, notes, due times, repeats; wall-clock alarms
App preferences Home screen focus, sounds, alert mode, biometric lock, screen-secure, Today filters
Optional media you choose Custom audio files you import for reminder sounds

This information is created by you (or generated locally from schedules you configure). It is used only to provide Rattle’s reminder, history, and settings features on your device(s).

Sensitive labels such as medication names, care-person names, and alarm names are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM with a key stored in the Android Keystore. Android backup / device-transfer of Rattle’s database and preferences is disabled in the app configuration.

3. Information we (the developer) collect

We do not collect personal information from Rattle through a developer-operated server.

In particular, Rattle as currently shipped:

  • Does not create a user account with the developer
  • Does not include third-party advertising SDKs
  • Does not include developer analytics / crash-reporting SDKs that upload your medication or care data
  • Does not sell your data

If this changes in a future version (for example optional cloud backup you turn on), this policy will be updated before that behavior ships, and the Play Data safety section will be updated to match.

Rattle does not need contacts, location, microphone, camera, SMS, or call-log access for its core features.

5. Wear OS companion

If you install the Wear companion and pair a watch, Rattle may send reminder payloads and sound-related data between your phone and your watch using Google Play services Wearable message / data APIs. That traffic is between your paired devices. It is not a submission of your health data to the Rattle developer.

6. Sharing

We do not share Rattle user content with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for marketing.

Your data may leave your device only in ways you or the platform initiate, for example:

  • You manually export, screenshot, or share content outside the app
  • You use Android backup / transfer features for apps generally (Rattle excludes its database and prefs from backup/transfer rules)
  • Google Play / Android system services process installs, updates, and crash reports according to Google’s privacy policy
  • Communication with a watch you paired, as described above

7. Children

Rattle is intended for adults who manage their own schedules or caregiving tasks. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not store another person’s health information in the app unless you have a lawful basis and authority to do so.

8. Security

  • Optional biometric app lock
  • Optional screen-secure flag (helps block screenshots / recent-app previews)
  • Encryption of selected sensitive strings via Android Keystore
  • Exclusion of app database and preferences from Android backup / device-transfer rules

No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. You are responsible for physical access to your unlocked device and for OS-level security updates.

9. Your choices

  • You can delete medications, care people, tasks, alarms, history entries, and custom sounds inside the app.
  • You can uninstall Rattle to remove its local data from that device (subject to normal Android uninstall behavior).
  • You can disable biometric lock, notifications, or Wear features in Settings / system settings.
  • Because the developer does not hold a cloud copy of your Rattle data, there is no cloud account deletion request to process for app content. Contact timothypcoleary@gmail.com for privacy questions about the policy itself.

10. International users

Rattle processes the information described above on the device where you install the app. The developer does not operate a Rattle content server that transfers that content internationally.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s privacy practices change. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised. Review the policy when you update the app.
